# Audit: 2026-09-12

Purpose: record what four independent reviews found, what was changed in response, and the honest status of every item that was not closed.

Status: reviews executed 2026-09-12, item status updated 2026-09-14 for model `0.2.0-screening`. Items 1 to 6 are closed, as are items 8 to 11 raised after the audit; item 7 (benchmark gap) remains open and is now blocked by an external absence rather than by unfinished work here.

Read this if: you are judging how much scrutiny this model has survived, or picking up an open item.

Four independent read-only reviews (model, data, interface, roadmap) plus a metric gap the client raised. Findings are cited to file:line at audit time; line numbers drift after fixes. The regression suite was 22 tests before the audit and 26 after it; it stands at 91 on 2026-09-14 (see [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md)).

The controller still runs the pad in hours the screen calls ineffective because partial cooling beats none under the violation-first rule; that is the intended reading of the two tables side by side.

## Found after the audit: 2026-09-14

Three further items surfaced in the waves after the audit, recorded in the same conventions. Ids continue the audit's letters: `M` model, `D` documentation or reporting convention, `G` a gap raised rather than a defect found.

| Id | Severity | Where | Finding | Fix | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M3 | Major | `src/simulate.js` light request | The lighting controller spread the outstanding daily light deficit over the remaining lit window plus half a control step, so every step asked for slightly too little. A fixture with ample capacity therefore finished every day fractionally short: an indoor farm delivering 13.993 of 14 mol was reported light-deficient on 365 of 365 days, which made the metric useless for exactly the indoor and hybrid classes that depend on it. | The request window is the time actually left in the civil day, floored at one step, so the final step asks for the outstanding deficit exactly. Delivery lands on the target and is step-invariant; an undersized fixture still reports its shortfall. Regenerating the regional study left every verdict and strategy order unchanged, no strategy moving more than 0.05 pp or 50 dollars. | `a fixture with surplus capacity reaches the daily light target and is not reported deficient`: a surplus fixture reaches its 20 mol target on every complete day at both 5 and 1 minute steps, and the same fixture against a 40 mol target still reports two deficit days at its 27.0 mol ceiling |
| D6 | Major | `src/metrics.js` design conditions | Design conditions were described in ASHRAE mean-coincident terms. ASHRAE obtains a mean coincident value by double-binning hourly data into joint frequency matrices and taking the conditional mean at the design condition, over a nominal 25-year station record; this tool reports the coincident state of the single ranked exceedance hour from ten reanalysis years, which is a different and noisier statistic. Raised by the evidence review ([EVIDENCE-HOT-HUMID.md](EVIDENCE-HOT-HUMID.md) §6). | The design-conditions basis string now states that these are not ASHRAE mean coincident values, names the double-binning difference and the record length, and states that latent equipment belongs to the dew-point family rather than to dry bulb with coincident wet bulb. The separate 0.4 / 1 / 2 percent dry-bulb, wet-bulb and dew-point exceedances were always computed, so the sizing error the naming implied was never present in the numbers. | n/a (reporting convention) |
| G2 | Gap (evidence review) | `src/screens.js` ventilation path | `maxVentACH` was a user input with no screen-dependent derate, so a user fitting fine insect mesh in the tropics got an optimistic ventilation answer unless they lowered the input themselves. The measured Thai campaign shows finer mesh cutting ventilation by about 35 and 50 percent while raising indoor moisture. | `insectScreen` derates the achievable maximum outside-air exchange by the measured ratios 1.000 / 0.641 / 0.502 for nominal 40, 52 and 78 mesh. The reference is the measured 40-mesh house and not an unscreened one, so the cost of the first screen stays UNSOURCED and an installed screen with no declared factor blocks the run instead of costing nothing. Minimum ventilation is a requirement rather than a capability, so it is not derated and a derate that would fall below it clamps and says so. | `an insect screen restricts the outside-air path and costs a vent-limited humid house both heat and moisture`; `an installed insect screen must declare what it costs, and a derate never falls below the required minimum` |

**Three external efficiency headlines dissolved on inspection**, recorded here because the pattern recurs and because this register is where a reader looks for what scrutiny found. A 25 percent liquid-desiccant electricity saving is measured apparatus against a *calibrated model* of the same system without desiccant, and its authors limit the claim themselves. A 26.2 percent membrane saving counts about 120 W of vacuum-pump duty allocated by the permeate-to-bypass flow ratio, not the 1.5 kW the pump actually draws. A 63 percent field improvement compares a measured EER 14.7 against an incumbent chiller's *rated* 9.0, not a measured chiller. None of the three was used to set a default in this repository. Full entries, with DOIs and grades, are in [EVIDENCE-HOT-HUMID.md](EVIDENCE-HOT-HUMID.md) §3.

## Open items: status at 2026-09-14

| # | Item | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Controller cadence dependence is structural | **Closed** by the staged deadband controller (v0.2). Measured 1 min vs 0.5 min: 0.004 / 0.383 / 0.091 pp attainment, 0.31% electricity max. The old optimizer remains as a labeled upper bound and still fails to converge. |
| 2 | Missing physical regressions | **Closed.** `test/conservation.test.mjs` adds six quantitative identities: pad water balance, indirect-evap secondary stream, humidifier latent cooling, free-running thermal-mass and infiltration trajectory against the closed-form exponential, DX coil and condenser identities as equalities, and dominance behaviour for disjoint eligible-hour sets and failed runs. Every expectation is derived independently of the value it checks. No defect was found: all identities close to between 1e-16 and 5e-13 relative, so this was a coverage gap rather than a physics error. |
| 3 | Timeline heatmap is colour-only | **Closed.** Every cell now carries a shape as well as a colour (solid block, lower half block, open cell with slash, centre dot), the legend and canvas label describe the shape first, and a keyboard-reachable day-by-hour table renders from the same rows so chart and table cannot disagree. Batched into one Path2D per bucket: a full year draws in about 7 ms. |
| 4 | Hour inspector has no live announcement | **Closed.** `aria-valuetext` on the slider plus one debounced polite status line; calendar and table selection both drive the same path. |
| 5 | Import parses on the main thread | **Closed.** Imports post the File to a parse worker; the misleading 512 MB file-size limit is replaced by an honest statement that the ceiling is the memory the tab can allocate, with staged progress. All prior guarantees kept, and the epoch re-check now covers CSV and bare weather JSON as well as run bundles. |
| 6 | Data vintages | **Closed.** `src/vintages.js` declares a per-dataset staleness budget with a stated rationale and `scripts/check-vintages.mjs` reports ages offline, exiting 1 when anything is past budget. Measured 2026-09-14: 4 current, 1 aging (eGRID 2023, 32 mo), 1 stale (utility mapping 2021, 56 mo, not fixable by `--refresh` because the source URLs are pinned to that year), 1 unknown (the EPA ZIP tool carries no machine-readable published date). CI reports it without failing the build. |
| 7 | Benchmark gap | **Open, externally blocked.** Roadmap M4/M5. The only named public dataset cannot exercise pad, DX or dehumidification paths, so the benchmark needs a model-to-model run on the same forcing. The evidence review then found the harder half: no independent hot-humid greenhouse benchmark dataset exists, and no greenhouse model reviewed has a published hot-humid latent validation ([EVIDENCE-HOT-HUMID.md](EVIDENCE-HOT-HUMID.md) §7). Closing this item as originally written is therefore not available at any effort we control; see [DIGITAL-TWIN.md](DIGITAL-TWIN.md). |
| 8 | Lighting under-delivery | **Closed** by M3 above. Delivery lands on the daily target exactly and is step-invariant at 5 and 1 minute; shortfalls of undersized fixtures are still reported. |
| 9 | Design conditions named as ASHRAE mean coincident | **Closed** by D6 above. The reported figure is the coincident state of the single ranked exceedance hour, and says so. |
| 10 | Insect-screen ventilation penalty not modeled | **Closed** by G2 above, with the first screen's cost left explicitly unsourced. |
| 11 | Three literature efficiency headlines rest on a simulated comparator, an allocated auxiliary-power boundary and a nameplate comparator | **Closed as recorded**, not actionable in code: none set a default here, and each is documented with its comparator boundary in [EVIDENCE-HOT-HUMID.md](EVIDENCE-HOT-HUMID.md) §3. The standing rule it produces: an efficiency claim is read at its comparator, not at its headline. |
