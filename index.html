02

Build a scenario

1 scenario

Picking a facility or a cultivation system loads a whole template of assumptions. Picking a technology replaces the equipment capacities and the installed-cost figures. You can edit every one of them below.

Outdoor air

Air that leaks in and air you bring in on purpose are two different things, and the tool keeps them apart. Fan specific power covers the supply and exhaust fans together. Air moving around inside the room, and how fast it passes over the canopy, are not modelled at all.

Mushroom outdoor air requires project inputs for species, growth stage, substrate loading, CO2 target and equipment. No universal minimum or maximum is assumed.

Literature context, not design limits
Heat recovery

The model always balances supply against exhaust, and it bypasses recovery in hours when raw outside air is already doing the job. Recovery treats only the outdoor air you control, never leakage. Enter performance from your own project or from the manufacturer, because no generic ratings ship with this tool.

Enter effectiveness as a fraction at 75 percent and at 100 percent of rated flow, given separately for heating and for cooling.

HRV latent effectiveness is fixed at zero.

Dry-neutral DOAS

Project or manufacturer inputs are required. Maximum treatment capacity is a subset of controlled outdoor air, not added airflow. Supply temperature and dew point, cooling COP and reheat recovery determine treatment energy.

The first scenario is what everything else gets measured against. A new strategy copies its non-equipment assumptions, so only the equipment differs. The installed costs here are illustrative figures, not quotes.

Sensitivity comparisons

Change one assumption at a time. The spread you get back shows how far that single input moves the answer. It is not a statistical confidence interval.

INVESTMENT SCREEN

More control, at what cost?

Every strategy is scored against the same historical record. Capital cost and running cost are kept apart and never added together. Added target hours are counted against the first scenario. They are not crop revenue and not an annual return.

CLIMATE RISK ACROSS YEARS

Median year, worst year, spread

joint attainment by year · median line

The same scenarios run against one weather year each. You get the middle year, the worst, the best, the gap between worst and best, and a trend line once five or more years are selected. Ten years of modelled weather tells you how much the answer moves between years. It is not a forecast.

ACROSS SITES

Which climate gives the most for free?

For each site: the hours when outside air alone could have done the cooling, the hours when an evaporative pad would genuinely work, which limit binds hardest, the cheapest strategy still worth considering, and how bad the worst year gets. Extra sites are priced at your manual scenario prices, not at their own local rates.

Your inputs, a record of where every source came from, and the hour-by-hour output. The design-basis brief pulls out the peak hours, the air and water requirements and the verdict, written for the engineer who will do the real design.

Know what this screen can tell you

TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Weather is evidence.

NASA POWER is a gridded reconstruction. Station mode pairs observed meteorology with separately sourced solar. Missing intervals remain visible, not replaced with favorable conditions.

Research & source register ↗

Equipment is an assumption.

Constant component capacities, efficiencies and simplified controls compare strategies. Crop evaporation, uniform air, glazing and heat recovery need site-specific review. These are not sizing or yield guarantees.

Evidence tiers & evaluation gates ↗

Technology, not brand rankings.

Desiccant and hybrid strategies are generic, editable screens. Blue Frontier and AGronomic IQ are technology references only. No validated product maps or manufacturer results are implied.
